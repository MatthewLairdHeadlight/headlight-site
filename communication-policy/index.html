Practice Policies

Please review these communication expectations.

These policies explain how to contact the practice, how appointment communication is handled, and what to expect from SMS messages.

Email

Our email is encrypted and HIPAA-compliant per Google Workspace's standards for healthcare providers, with a Business Associate Agreement on file.

Social Media

Please do not connect with, follow, or message any of our staff via personal or public social media accounts. We do not respond to communications from established patients through social media.

Appointments: No-Shows, Late Arrivals, and Cancellations

We work hard to respect your time and keep appointments running smoothly, and we ask patients to do the same. If you no-show, arrive significantly late, or cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, you'll receive a reminder of this policy through the patient portal. A repeat occurrence puts this policy into effect, which may result in termination from the practice. If terminated, you'll receive a certified letter and a final 30-day medication supply/refill to allow time to establish care elsewhere.

SMS Communication Consent

By scheduling an appointment, you agree to receive SMS messages related to booking and appointment follow-up from Headlight Mental Healthcare. Message frequency may vary; message/data rates may apply. Text HELP to (480) 618-7023 or (541) 581-1080 for assistance. Text STOP to opt out at any time.
