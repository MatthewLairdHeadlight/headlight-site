About Headlight

A calmer way to find the right care

Headlight Mental Healthcare was founded on a simple belief: getting help should never be the hardest part of getting better. We removed the long waitlists, the phone-tag and the paperwork mazes, and replaced them with a clear path from first call to first session.

Our multidisciplinary team of therapists, psychiatric providers and care coordinators works together on one plan — yours. You will never have to repeat your story to five different people.

Three steps, no maze
