What Is It?

A smarter way to find the right medication

GeneSight is an FDA-cleared pharmacogenomic test that analyzes how your unique genetic makeup may affect the way your body processes dozens of psychiatric medications.

A simple cheek swab — done right here in the office — is all it takes. The lab analyzes genetic markers linked to drug metabolism, and within days Matthew reviews the results with you. Each medication is categorized into a clear green / yellow / red framework based on your DNA, so your treatment plan starts with a foundation of real genetic evidence rather than guesswork.

Clinical Evidence

How Does It Help?

Pharmacogenomic-guided prescribing is not experimental — it is backed by large-scale clinical research.

The GUIDED Clinical Trial

One of the largest pharmacogenomics studies ever conducted, the GUIDED trial demonstrated a significantly higher remission rate for patients whose medications were selected using GeneSight results compared to those prescribed by traditional methods alone.

Reduce Trial & Error

Finding the right psychiatric medication without genetic data can mean months of adjustments, unwanted side effects, and frustration. GeneSight helps narrow the field from the very first appointment, saving valuable time.

Personalized for You

Two patients with the same diagnosis may respond very differently to the same medication. Your genetics are unique — your treatment plan should be too. GeneSight provides the data to make that personalization possible.

The Process

Simple. Fast. Informative.

From swab to personalized treatment plan in just a few steps.

  1. 1

    Simple Cheek Swab

    During your appointment, Matthew collects a painless cheek swab. No blood draw, no lab visit — it takes under a minute.

  2. 2

    Lab Analyzes Your Genetics

    The sample is sent directly to the GeneSight lab, where scientists analyze the genetic markers that influence how your body metabolizes psychiatric medications. Results typically return within a few days.

  3. 3

    Matthew Reviews Results With You

    Matthew walks through your personalized report — which medications fall in the green, yellow, or red category for you — and works with you to build a treatment plan grounded in your unique biology.
