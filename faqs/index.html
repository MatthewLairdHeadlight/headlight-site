Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about getting started, appointments, insurance, medication management, and practice policies.

FAQ Categories

Getting Started

Who does Headlight Mental Healthcare serve?

Headlight Mental Healthcare serves adults (18+) seeking psychiatric medication management in Arizona and Oregon, either in person or via telehealth. The practice does not currently provide therapy or counseling services.

Do you accept new patients?

Yes. New patients can request an appointment by completing the secure IntakeQ intake form on the Contact page. Matthew reviews all requests personally and will respond within one business day.

What is the intake process like?

Complete the secure IntakeQ new-patient form with basic background information and your reason for seeking care. Once reviewed, you will receive a confirmation and scheduling information. Your first appointment is a comprehensive psychiatric evaluation, typically 60–75 minutes.

Do you provide therapy or counseling?

No. Headlight Mental Healthcare focuses exclusively on psychiatric medication evaluation and management. Therapy referrals are encouraged and can be coordinated with your prescribing care.

Appointments & Scheduling

How do I schedule an appointment?

Use the secure intake form on the Contact page to request an appointment. Direct scheduling is not available before the initial intake is reviewed.

How long are appointments?

Initial evaluations are typically 60–75 minutes. Follow-up medication management appointments are generally 20–30 minutes, depending on complexity.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment. Late cancellations and no-shows may be subject to a fee. See the full details in our Communication Policy.

How quickly can I be seen?

Availability varies. After your intake form is reviewed you will receive scheduling options. Matthew aims to respond to all new patient inquiries within one business day.

Medication Management

What psychiatric conditions do you treat?

Common conditions include depression, anxiety disorders, bipolar disorder, ADHD, PTSD, OCD, and other psychiatric conditions that respond to medication. A full evaluation is required before any treatment plan is established.

Will I be prescribed medication at my first appointment?

Not necessarily. The initial appointment is a comprehensive psychiatric evaluation. Treatment recommendations, including whether medication is appropriate, are made after a thorough assessment.

How do I request a prescription refill?

Refills are managed at scheduled follow-up appointments. Please do not wait until you run out — plan ahead and request refills with adequate lead time. Refills outside of scheduled appointments are handled at the provider's discretion and are not guaranteed.

Do you prescribe controlled substances?

Controlled substances (such as stimulants for ADHD or certain sleep or anxiety medications) may be prescribed when clinically appropriate and in compliance with applicable state and federal regulations. Prescriptions for controlled substances require an established patient relationship and regular follow-up.

What is GeneSight testing?

GeneSight is a pharmacogenomic test that analyzes how your genes may affect your response to certain psychiatric medications. It can help guide medication selection. Learn more on the GeneSight page.

Telehealth

Is telehealth available?

Yes. Telehealth appointments are available for patients in Arizona and Oregon. Telehealth is conducted through a HIPAA-compliant video platform.

What do I need for a telehealth appointment?

A device with a camera and microphone (smartphone, tablet, or computer), a stable internet connection, and a private location are all that is required. You will receive a link or instructions before your appointment.

Can I be seen via telehealth if I live outside Arizona or Oregon?

No. Matthew Laird, PMHNP-BC is licensed in Arizona and Oregon. Patients must be physically located in one of those states at the time of service.

Policies

What is the communication policy?

Headlight Mental Healthcare communicates through a secure patient portal. Phone messages are returned during business hours. Email is not used for clinical communications. For full details, review the Communication Policy.

Is this practice a good fit if I am in crisis?

Headlight Mental Healthcare is an outpatient practice and is not equipped to manage psychiatric emergencies. If you are in crisis, call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or 911, or go to your nearest emergency room.

What if I have a question not answered here?

Contact the office directly using the information on the Contact page. Matthew answers his own messages.
