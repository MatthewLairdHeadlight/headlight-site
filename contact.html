Before you call

Common questions

Do you take my insurance?

We are in-network with most major commercial plans and several Medicaid programs. Send us your plan name and member ID and we will verify your benefits before your first visit.

How soon can I be seen?

Most new clients have a first appointment within three to five business days. Telehealth slots are often available sooner.

Can I switch clinicians?

Absolutely. Fit matters more than anything else in therapy — tell your care coordinator and we will re-match you, no awkward conversation required.
