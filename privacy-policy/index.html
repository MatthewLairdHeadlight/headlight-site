Privacy Policy for Headlight Mental Healthcare

At Headlight Mental Healthcare, we value your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, protect, and share your personal information, as well as your rights regarding your personal data. By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Collection and Storage of Personal Information

We collect personal information to provide you with the best possible care and services. The types of personal information we may collect include:

  • Contact Information: Name, address, phone number, and email address.
  • Health Information: Medical history, treatment details, and mental health assessments.
  • Payment Information: Billing address, credit card details, and insurance information (if applicable).
  • Communication Information: Correspondence with you through phone calls, emails, or messages.

Payments and Stripe

We use Stripe to securely process payments for our services. When you choose to pay by credit card, debit card, or directly from a bank account, Stripe acts as our payment processor and service provider. Stripe may collect and process limited financial information, such as tokenized payment card details or bank account identifiers, account ownership information, balances, and transaction data, as needed to process payments, prevent fraud, and support our internal accounting and reconciliation. We do not receive or store your online banking login credentials. We do not sell this financial data or use it for marketing. We share it only with Stripe and other service providers as necessary to provide our services and to comply with legal and regulatory requirements. For more information about how Stripe handles personal information, please see Stripe's privacy documentation at https://stripe.com/privacy.

2. Use and Sharing of Personal Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Provide mental healthcare services, including appointments, treatment plans, and follow-up.
  • Process payments and insurance claims (if applicable).
  • Communicate with you regarding appointments, treatment progress, and other necessary updates.
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

Sharing of Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent, except in the following cases:

  • To healthcare providers, insurance companies, or third-party partners for billing or treatment coordination purposes.
  • To comply with legal requirements, such as court orders or subpoenas.
  • For medical emergencies or as required by law to protect your health and safety.

We will never share your SMS consent or phone number with any third-party vendors or affiliates for marketing or commercial purposes.

3. Protection of Personal Information

We take the protection of your personal information seriously. We have implemented physical, administrative, and technical measures to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include, but are not limited to:

  • Secure servers and encryption methods.
  • Restricted access to authorized personnel only.
  • Regular audits and updates to our security practices.

Despite these efforts, please understand that no data transmission over the internet or data storage system can be guaranteed 100% secure. If you have concerns about the security of your personal information, please contact us directly.
