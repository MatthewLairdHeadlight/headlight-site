A Tool of Precision Care

Your genetics, informing your medication.

Finding the right psychiatric medication can feel like trial and error — because without genetic information, it often is. GeneSight is a simple cheek-swab test that analyzes how your genes interact with dozens of mental health medications, placing each in a green / yellow / red category so Matthew can personalize your treatment from the start. In the GUIDED clinical trial, patients had a significantly higher remission rate when their medications were guided by GeneSight results compared to traditional prescribing, and insurance often covers the test.
