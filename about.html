Professional bio

Respectful care for people navigating complex symptoms

Matthew Laird, PMHNP-BC is a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner focused on helping adults feel safer, steadier, and more informed about their options for treatment.

His work centers on anxiety, depression, OCD, ADHD, mood disorders, and medication optimization — including genetic testing evaluation when it can support better decision-making. The tone of care remains direct, compassionate, and grounded in clinical evidence.

Patients can expect careful listening, practical follow-through, and a treatment relationship built on dignity rather than pressure.
