Services

Whole-person psychiatric care

Headlight Mental Healthcare offers a focused set of psychiatric services centered on common, serious, and often co-occurring mental health concerns.

◎

Anxiety

Support for persistent worry, panic, overactivation, and the physical strain that anxiety can place on work, sleep, and relationships.

◌

Depression

Care for low mood, loss of motivation, exhaustion, hopelessness, and the everyday functioning changes depression can create.

◍

OCD

Clinical attention to obsessions, compulsions, mental rituals, and the stress that follows when symptoms begin to take over more of life.

◔

ADHD

Evaluation and treatment planning for attention, focus, organization, and the way ADHD can intersect with anxiety, mood, and burnout.

◑

Mood Disorders

Support for mood instability, recurring emotional shifts, and the need for careful, individualized medication and symptom review.

✦

Genetic Testing / Medication Optimization

GeneSight-informed medication review can help guide conversations about past medication responses, side effects, and next-step options.

◆

Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) for Addiction & Co-Occurring Conditions

MAT is offered with dignity and clinical seriousness by a board-certified, MAT-credentialed provider. Treatment is integrated with psychiatric care because addiction often co-occurs with anxiety, depression, ADHD, and mood symptoms.

Clinical approach

Serious about complexity, never casual about care

  • Board-certified psychiatric evaluation and follow-up.
  • Respectful treatment planning for co-occurring symptoms and diagnoses.
  • Medication decisions grounded in evidence, function, and lived experience.
