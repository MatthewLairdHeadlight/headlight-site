Billing Partner — Expectations & Accountability

One-page service expectations to improve responsiveness, follow-through, and traceable outcomes.

Response SLA

  • Acknowledge every request (call/text/email) within [X business hours].
  • Provide resolution or a clear status update within [Y business days].
  • If blocked, state exactly what is needed and from whom.

Resolution Reporting

  • Close each request with a written outcome.
  • Required closeout: what was done, what's pending, next steps.
  • No silent drops or unresolved “I’ll look into it.”

Chart-Based Work Standard

  • Any audit/claim review must reference the actual chart/EHR record.
  • Document which encounters were reviewed.
  • No “audit complete” without chart-level verification notes.

Weekly Summary

  • Send a brief weekly status report.
  • Include open items, denials worked, and resolutions completed.
  • Highlight items needing provider decision/action.

Escalation Rule

Any item unresolved past SLA is escalated/flagged for immediate review with date, reason, and owner.
