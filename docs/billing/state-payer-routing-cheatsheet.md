# State & Payer Routing — Bill Remote Visits Where They Pay Best

## Key Fact
Cigna and Blue Cross Blue Shield separate enrollment by **state/tax ID**. Oregon and Arizona are distinct entities with distinct fee schedules.

- Oregon reimburses higher than Arizona for the same services.
- Provider performs both telehealth and in-person care.
- Remote patients are associated with both state entities.

## Optimization Focus
For telehealth claims, ensure they route to the higher-paying state entity where appropriate/allowed.

- Verify DrChrono billing profiles and provider enrollment are sending remote-Oregon claims under the Oregon tax ID/fee schedule.
- Make sure remote visits are not defaulting to Arizona if Oregon routing is valid and compliant.

## Question for Biller
**“For our remote/telehealth patients, are claims being routed to the correct (and highest-paying, where allowed) state entity — Oregon vs Arizona? Please confirm remote-Oregon visits bill under the Oregon tax ID/fee schedule.”**

## Compliance Caution
Claim routing must follow each payer's telehealth location rules (often based on where the patient is located). Confirm compliance with biller — do not misroute claims.
