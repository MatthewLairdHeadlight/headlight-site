A Life Beyond the Clinic

The Person, Not Just the Provider

When I'm not in clinic, you'll find me on a trail, on my bike, or somewhere outdoors — moving, breathing, and remembering why all of this matters. I've spent the last decade building a life that looks a lot like the one I encourage my patients toward: connected, active, full of imperfect moments and genuinely good ones.

I got into psychiatric nursing because I believe people can change — and because I've watched it happen, again and again, when someone finally feels truly heard. That hasn't gotten old in twenty years.
